# Scaling Guide

## Formula

```
Max concurrent sink calls = goose_pods × workers_per_pod
                              (capped by Kafka partitions)

Max throughput = concurrent_calls × (1000 / endpoint_latency_ms)
```

<Callout type="info">
  Measured per-pod throughput (2026-09-28, 1 pod, 1 partition): ~6,000/s at 0ms sink delay and ~4,000/s at 50ms with `WORKER_POOL_SIZE=100, MAX_POLL_RECORDS=500`. Ordered mode (`POOL=1`) runs ~800/s. See [Load Test Results](/benchmarking/load-test-results) for the full report.
</Callout>

## Example Configurations

The per-pod numbers below use actual benchmarked throughput (50ms sink delay, multi-worker mode). Scale by pods up to the partition count.

| Partitions | Pods | Workers | Latency | Throughput (approx) |
|-----------|------|---------|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | 1 | 100 | 50ms | ~4,000/s |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 0ms (ordered) | ~800/s |
| 3 | 3 | 100 | 50ms | ~12,000/s |
| 10 | 10 | 100 | 50ms | ~40,000/s |
| 10 | 10 | 100 | 0ms | ~60,000/s |

## K8s Resource Limits

```yaml
resources:
  requests:
    cpu: 200m
    memory: 64Mi
  limits:
    cpu: 500m
    memory: 128Mi
```

## Checklist

- [ ] Kafka partitions ≥ desired max pods
- [ ] `SINK_WORKER_POOL_SIZE` matches CPU limits
- [ ] `SINK_HTTP_MAX_CONNECTIONS` = `SINK_WORKER_POOL_SIZE` (auto-matched)
- [ ] `SOURCE_KAFKA_CONSUMER_CONFIG_MAX_POLL_RECORDS` × HTTP_latency < `SOURCE_KAFKA_MAX_POLL_INTERVAL_MS`
